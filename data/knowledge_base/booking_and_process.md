# Booking a Site Visit & Our Process

1. You tell us roughly what you need (number of cameras, type of property,
   area/location).
2. We schedule a free site assessment, usually within 24-48 hours of your
   request, Monday-Saturday.
3. Our technician visits, confirms camera placement, cable routing and any
   special requirements (e.g. night vision range, storage retention period).
4. We send a written quotation (PDF) by email or WhatsApp within 24 hours of
   the site visit.
5. On approval, installation is typically completed within 1-3 working days
   depending on scope.
6. We provide a 12-month warranty on installation workmanship and pass
   through manufacturer warranty on hardware.

To book a site visit, a customer just needs to share their location and
preferred day - our team will confirm a time slot.
