# Pricing & Delivery

We don't publish fixed prices publicly because every installation is different
(number of cameras, cable runs, whether trenching is needed, etc). Instead we
offer a **free site assessment** and send a written quotation within 24 hours.

As a rough starting point:
- A basic 4-camera home CCTV package (analog, 1080p) typically starts around
  KES 45,000 installed, including a DVR and 1TB hard drive.
- IP camera packages with remote phone viewing typically start around
  KES 65,000 for 4 cameras.
- Final pricing always depends on cable distance, number of cameras, and
  whether trenching/conduit work is required.

We do not do nationwide courier delivery of equipment on its own - our model
is installation-inclusive. We deliver and install anywhere in the greater
Nairobi metro area (Nairobi, Kiambu, Kajiado border towns like Rongai/Ngong,
Machakos border towns like Athi River).
