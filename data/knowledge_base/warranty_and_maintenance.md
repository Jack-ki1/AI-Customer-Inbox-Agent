# Warranty, Maintenance & Support

- All installations come with a 12-month workmanship warranty.
- Cameras and recorders carry their manufacturer's hardware warranty
  (typically 12-24 months depending on brand).
- We offer optional annual maintenance contracts which include: lens
  cleaning, cable inspection, firmware updates, and 2 free callouts per year.
- For urgent faults outside a maintenance contract, we charge a standard
  callout fee plus parts if needed.
- Remote viewing app support (setup on your phone) is included free with
  every installation.
